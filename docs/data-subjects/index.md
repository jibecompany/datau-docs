# End Users / Data Subjects

This section is for **citizens** who take part in SPOON studies. As a data subject you are not a
passive source of data — you are an active participant who decides how your data is used.

Everything here is built on **data sovereignty**: your personal data stays under your control at all
times, is shared only with your explicit consent, and is anonymised before any research team can see
it.

## Your data, your rights

Whichever tool you use, the same guarantees apply:

- **Consent-based collection** — your data is shared only when you explicitly consent, at the moment
  you save each data item.
- **Anonymisation** — personal identifiers (such as names and addresses) are replaced with
  anonymised tokens before storage. Researchers never see the real values.
- **GDPR compliance** — the platform implements GDPR-rooted controls: access, rectification,
  erasure, and portability.
- **Single sign-on** — one DataU account works across every SPOON tool, managed securely by Keycloak
  (OpenID Connect) at `auth.datau.jibe.cloud`.
