# SPOON Project

## What is SPOON?

**SPOON** (Sustainable Personalised cOnsumer fOod behaviour ecosystem on Nutrition) is a Horizon
Europe citizen-science project investigating food-consumption behaviour and the transition toward
healthier and more sustainable diets across the EU. It combines empirical research, citizen-driven
data collection, and the development of a shared digital infrastructure that lets researchers,
citizens, and the food chain interact under clear data-governance rules.

The project sits at the intersection of three domains that have historically lived in separate silos:
consumer behaviour research, food-chain data, and the EU's evolving data governance and sovereignty
agenda. SPOON is one of the first large consortia to attempt to unify these in production rather than
in theory.

## Mission

Generate trustworthy, citizen-contributed evidence on how people actually eat — and use that evidence
to inform interventions, policy, and food-system design — while keeping data subjects in control of
their personal information and giving partners a sustainable infrastructure to keep building on after
the project ends.

## The DataU platform

DataU is the digital backbone of SPOON, developed and operated by **Jibe Company BV** as WP2 lead. It
is a layered platform stack that all consortium partners use to run studies, collect data, and
surface results. Jibe owns all DataU software components; partners own their questionnaires and
datasets; data subjects retain inalienable GDPR rights over their personal data.

## Data governance principles

SPOON's data governance is built on three inalienable principles, reflecting the EU's data
sovereignty agenda:

- **Citizen ownership** — data subjects retain full GDPR rights over their personal data regardless
  of any consent given for processing. Consent is a lawful basis for processing only — never a
  transfer of ownership.
- **Partner content ownership** — partners own the questionnaire content they author and the
  datasets they generate. Jibe hosts that content under an operational licence, but hosting does not
  change ownership.
- **Platform ownership** — all DataU software tools and infrastructure are Jibe-owned. Jibe's
  ownership persists after the SPOON project ends.
