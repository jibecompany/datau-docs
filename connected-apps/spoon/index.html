    SPOON Project¶

    What is SPOON?¶

    SPOON (Sustainable Personalised cOnsumer fOod behaviour ecosystem on Nutrition) is a Horizon Europe citizen-science project investigating food-consumption behaviour and the transition toward healthier and more sustainable diets across the EU. It combines empirical research, citizen-driven data collection, and the development of a shared digital infrastructure that lets researchers, citizens, and the food chain interact under clear data-governance rules.

    The project sits at the intersection of three domains that have historically lived in separate silos: consumer behaviour research, food-chain data, and the EU's evolving data governance and sovereignty agenda. SPOON is one of the first large consortia to attempt to unify these in production rather than in theory.

    Mission¶

    Generate trustworthy, citizen-contributed evidence on how people actually eat — and use that evidence to inform interventions, policy, and food-system design — while keeping data subjects in control of their personal information and giving partners a sustainable infrastructure to keep building on after the project ends.

    The SPOON toolset¶

    The SPOON toolset is the suite of applications you use to take part. They are built on the DataU platform, so every interaction — logging in, answering a questionnaire, sharing a data item — is governed by consent and data sovereignty.

    Programme & consortium¶

    About the project

    • Programme: Horizon Europe — HORIZON-CL6-2024-FARM2FORK-01
    • Grant Agreement: 101182299
    • Consortium: 16 partners across the EU
    • Coordinator: Arlind Xhelili (CSCP — Collaborating Centre on Sustainable Consumption and Production)
    • Duration: 48 months (project end late 2028)
    • Jibe's role: Work Package 2 lead — Development and Deployment of Digital Tools and Data Management

    The DataU platform¶

    DataU is the digital backbone of SPOON, developed and operated by Jibe Company BV as WP2 lead. It is a layered platform stack that all consortium partners use to run studies, collect data, and surface results. Jibe owns all DataU software components; partners own their questionnaires and datasets; data subjects retain inalienable GDPR rights over their personal data.

    Data governance principles¶

    SPOON's data governance is built on three inalienable principles, reflecting the EU's data sovereignty agenda:

    • Citizen ownership — data subjects retain full GDPR rights over their personal data regardless of any consent given for processing. Consent is a lawful basis for processing only — never a transfer of ownership.
    • Partner content ownership — partners own the questionnaire content they author and the datasets they generate. Jibe hosts that content under an operational licence, but hosting does not change ownership.
    • Platform ownership — all DataU software tools and infrastructure are Jibe-owned. Jibe's ownership persists after the SPOON project ends.
