Your data. Your rules.

DataU puts people in control of their personal data. The data is shared only with explicit consent, stays under the individual's control at all times, and gives developers a clean, consent-first way to build on top of it.

Built on data sovereignty

A small set of inalienable principles underpins everything DataU does.

{% include ".icons/material/account-heart.svg" %}

Individual ownership

Data subjects keep full GDPR rights over their personal data. Consent is a lawful basis for processing only — never a transfer of ownership.

{% include ".icons/material/check-decagram.svg" %}

Consent-based sharing

Nothing is shared without explicit consent, given at the moment each data item is saved. No silent background collection.

{% include ".icons/material/scale-balance.svg" %}

GDPR-rooted controls

Access, rectification and erasure are first-class — built into the platform, not bolted on.

{% include ".icons/material/key-variant.svg" %}

Single sign-on

One identity (Keycloak / OpenID Connect) works across every connected tool, so users authenticate once and move freely.
